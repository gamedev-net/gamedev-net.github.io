GameDev.net has been under a DDoS attack since approximately 11:30pm PST on April 28, 2025. Traffic levels have exceeded 10,000x normal daily traffic, and unfortunately no major service provider includes DDoS management without significant costs. The cloud host, Google Cloud, has been completely unhelpful thus far. Such is the world we live in.

This selfish act of one individual has not only crippled this Internet resource of 26 years, but costs incurred from the attack have potentially made GameDev.net unviable to sustain - assuming the attack stops.

I sincerely apologize for this, but I don't know if I can continue GameDev.net this way.

Unfortunately, the world has plenty of bad actors, and this individual is one of them (demanding a ransom of $10,000 USD). There once was a time GameDev.net could survive this sort of attack, but financially I've personally sustained GameDev.net for quite some time, and this is the type of behavior that makes it unsustainable for me.

I wish I had better news. If the site cannot be revived due to this malicious act, then I will ensure preservation of data in a place it can live on and be picked up by others (servers are still there, data is still there - just blocked). I'm considering all options at this point.

Update: I don't know what the future of GameDev.net will be. The bill is large, and I don't know how bad the folks at Google Cloud want their thousands of USD in charges from the last day. This page is a Github page just to keep the charges from stacking up further.

I'll update here when it's figured out. Either way, I'm sorry for this. I hope 26 years of GameDev.net doesn't end this way for all the thousands of folks around the world who still use and appreciate it. Destruction benefits no one.

Update 2: Working to bring GameDev.net back with assistance from Cloudflare. Hope to have the site back online soon. Google charges are still a major issue (approaching $4k for one day of this) but pending response from Google Billing.
